## 1. Evolution of Architecture Research

### Historical Analysis of Indian Tech Transformation

**Timeline Research:**
- **2000-2010**: Monolithic era dominated by service companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro)
- **2010-2015**: SOA experiments with heavy ESB implementations 
- **2015-2020**: Microservices adoption led by Flipkart, Paytm, Zomato
- **2020-2025**: Cloud-native and AI-first architectures

**Key Architectural Shifts:**
1. **Monolith → Microservices**: Flipkart's Big Billion Day failures drove this transition
2. **On-premise → Cloud**: Jio's digital transformation accelerated cloud adoption
3. **Request-Response → Event-Driven**: IRCTC and UPI led event-driven patterns
4. **Human-Centric → AI-Native**: All major Indian platforms now AI-first

**Statistical Analysis:**
- Indian IT services revenue: $194 billion (2023)
- Microservices adoption in Indian enterprises: 67% (2024)
- Cloud adoption rate: 89% of Indian companies (2025)
- AI integration: 78% of Indian tech companies (2025)

### UPI Innovation Case Study

**Technical Deep Dive:**

- Multi-layer fraud detection using graph analysis

**Global Impact:**
