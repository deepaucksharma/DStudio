## Interview Integration Strategy

### Format Structure:
1. **Context Setting**: Brief introduction to expert's background
2. **Current State Analysis**: Expert's view on present technology landscape
3. **Future Vision**: Predictions and roadmap for next decade
4. **India-Specific Insights**: Role of Indian innovation and market
5. **Technical Deep Dive**: Specific architecture or implementation details
6. **Career Advice**: Guidance for aspiring engineers and architects

### Audio Integration:
- **Pre-recorded Segments**: High-quality studio interviews
- **Live Call-ins**: Real-time expert participation
- **Voice Messages**: Community expert contributions
- **Panel Discussions**: Multiple experts on specific topics

### Cultural Adaptation:
- **Language Flexibility**: Hindi-English mix as per expert comfort
- **Mumbai References**: Local analogies and examples
- **Indian Context**: Relating global trends to Indian scenarios
- **Practical Applications**: Focus on implementable solutions

### Technical Authenticity:
- **Code Examples**: Experts sharing actual implementations
- **Architecture Diagrams**: Visual explanations during discussion
- **Performance Metrics**: Real-world system statistics
- **Lessons Learned**: Practical experiences and failures

This comprehensive interview plan ensures diverse perspectives while maintaining technical depth and cultural authenticity for Episode 100's milestone celebration.
