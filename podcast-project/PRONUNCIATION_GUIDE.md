## 🎙️ RECORDING STUDIO INSTRUCTIONS

### Voice Artist Guidelines
- **Pace**: Slightly slower than normal Hindi conversation
- **Emphasis**: Technical terms should be pronounced clearly and deliberately
- **Tone**: Conversational Mumbai street style, not academic
- **Breathing**: Pause after complex technical explanations
- **Energy**: Maintain enthusiasm throughout

### Recording Quality Standards
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1 kHz minimum
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit minimum
- **Format**: WAV uncompressed
- **Room Tone**: Record 30 seconds before each session
- **Retakes**: Mark pronunciation corrections clearly

### Audio Editing Notes
- **Technical Terms**: Leave 0.5s pause before/after for emphasis
- **Hindi Script**: Use for backup pronunciation reference
- **English Words**: Pronounce with slight Indian accent, not full American
- **Numbers**: Always use Indian numbering (लाख, करोड़)
