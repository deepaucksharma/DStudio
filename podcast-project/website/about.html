Our Journey

From a small recording setup in Mumbai to India's favorite tech podcast. Here's how we grew from 0 to 50,000+ weekly listeners.

Jan
2023

The Idea is Born

Rohit was explaining microservices to a junior engineer and realized: "This is too complicated. What if I explained it like Mumbai's local train network?" That evening, he called Priya with the podcast idea.

💡 Idea
Mar
2023

First Episode Launch

Episode 001: "CAP Theorem Explained Using Mumbai Local Trains" went live. Recorded in Rohit's Bandra apartment with a ₹5,000 microphone. 47 people listened to the full 45-minute episode.

🎙️ Episode 1 47 Listeners
Jun
2023

Growing Community

Hit 1,000 weekly listeners! Started our Discord server when engineers began asking follow-up questions. Priya joined as co-host after her Paytm database episode went viral on LinkedIn.

📈 1K Listeners 💬 Discord Launch
Sep
2023

First Mumbai Meetup

Organized our first in-person meetup at WeWork BKC. 73 engineers showed up to discuss "Database Sharding like Mumbai's Railway Zones". The energy was incredible - we knew we were onto something special.

🤝 First Meetup 73 Attendees
Dec
2023

10K Milestone

Reached 10,000 weekly listeners! Arjun and Kavya joined the team. Started the 3-hour deep-dive format that became our signature. Episode 025 on Flipkart's microservices got 25,000 downloads in the first week.

🎯 10K Listeners 👥 Team Expansion
Apr
2024

Multi-City Expansion

Hosted meetups in Bangalore, Delhi, and Hyderabad. 25,000 Discord members! Anil and Deepika joined to handle security content and community management. Started partnerships with Indian tech companies.

🏙️ Multi-City 25K Discord
Aug
2024

2 Million Downloads

Crossed 2 million total downloads! Episode 075 on "UPI Architecture" became our most popular episode with 150K downloads. Started getting speaking invitations from major tech conferences across India.

🎉 2M Downloads 🏆 Conference Talks
Aug
2025

Today: Mumbai's Favorite Tech Podcast

50,000+ weekly listeners across 150+ cities. Episode 091 just launched! Our community has helped 1000+ engineers crack interviews at top companies. We're still that same team, just with better microphones and more chai.

🚀 50K Listeners 🌟 Episode 91
