Paytm learned this lesson hard way. Diwali 2018, no circuit breakers, payment service kept retrying failed bank connections. Result: 50 million retry requests in 10 minutes, complete system meltdown, ₹45 crores lost.

The magic: Takes just 2 milliseconds to check circuit status, but saves hours of downtime."
