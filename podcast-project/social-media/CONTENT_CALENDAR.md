## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC STRATEGIES

### 1. TWITTER/X STRATEGY
**Daily Activity**: 4-6 tweets/day
**Optimal Times**: 9 AM, 1 PM, 6 PM, 9 PM IST
**Content Mix**:
- 30% Episode promotion
- 25% Tech tips in Hindi
- 20% Indian startup news commentary
- 15% Mumbai metaphors for tech concepts
- 10% Community engagement/polls

**Weekly Schedule**:
- **Monday**: "Monday Motivation" - Career tips in Hindi
- **Tuesday**: "Tech Tuesday" - Deep dive threads
- **Wednesday**: "Wednesday Wisdom" - Industry insights
- **Thursday**: "Throwback Thursday" - Tech history with Indian context
- **Friday**: "Friday Focus" - Weekend project ideas
- **Saturday**: "Saturday Stories" - Success stories from Indian tech
- **Sunday**: "Sunday Reflection" - Week recap and next week preview

### 2. LINKEDIN STRATEGY
**Daily Activity**: 1-2 posts/day
**Optimal Times**: 8 AM, 12 PM, 5 PM IST
**Content Focus**:
- Professional development for Indian IT professionals
- Career growth in Indian tech ecosystem
- Company case studies (Flipkart, Paytm, Zomato, etc.)
- Technical leadership insights
- Podcast episode highlights with key takeaways

**Weekly Themes**:
- **Monday**: "Career Monday" - Growth tips
- **Wednesday**: "Wisdom Wednesday" - Industry insights
- **Friday**: "Feature Friday" - Company spotlights

### 3. INSTAGRAM STRATEGY
**Daily Activity**: 1-2 posts/day + 3-5 stories
**Optimal Times**: 11 AM, 2 PM, 7 PM IST
**Content Types**:
- **Feed Posts**: Visual episode summaries, infographics, carousel posts
- **Stories**: Behind-the-scenes, quick tips, polls, Q&A
- **Reels**: 30-60 second tech explanations with Mumbai references
- **IGTV**: Extended episode highlights (5-10 minutes)

**Visual Style**:
- Mumbai-inspired color palette (blues, oranges, whites)
- Consistent typography with Hindi/English mix
- Local landmarks in background images
- Code snippet carousels with syntax highlighting

### 4. YOUTUBE SHORTS STRATEGY
**Upload Schedule**: 2-3 Shorts/week
**Optimal Times**: 6 PM, 8 PM IST
**Content Categories**:
- **Tech Tips**: Quick coding solutions (45 seconds)
- **Mumbai Metaphors**: Explaining complex concepts (60 seconds)
- **Industry News**: Latest updates with Hindi commentary (30 seconds)
- **Success Stories**: Indian startup journeys (60 seconds)

### 5. WHATSAPP BROADCAST STRATEGY
**Broadcast Schedule**: Daily at 9 AM IST
**Subscriber Target**: 10,000+ by March 2025
**Content Types**:
- **Monday**: Episode release notification with teaser
- **Tuesday**: Quick tech tip of the day
- **Wednesday**: Community poll or quiz
- **Thursday**: Industry news summary
- **Friday**: Weekend project suggestion
- **Saturday**: Behind-the-scenes content
- **Sunday**: Week ahead preview
