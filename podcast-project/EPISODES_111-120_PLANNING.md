Episodes 111-120 will focus on technologies where India is becoming a global leader and areas critical for India's digital transformation.

## 📚 EPISODE TOPICS

### Episode 111: Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)
**Focus**: UPI, Aadhaar, DigiLocker, ONDC architecture
**Why Important**: India's DPI is becoming a global model
**Indian Examples**: UPI's 10B+ monthly transactions, Aadhaar's 1.3B users
**Technical Deep Dive**: Scale, security, interoperability

### Episode 112: Voice & Vernacular AI
**Focus**: Multilingual NLP, voice interfaces, Bhashini project
**Why Important**: 500M+ Indians coming online need vernacular tech
**Indian Examples**: Google's Hindi voice search, ShareChat's regional content
**Technical Deep Dive**: Indic language models, voice synthesis

### Episode 113: Autonomous Vehicle Infrastructure
**Focus**: Self-driving tech for Indian conditions
**Why Important**: Ola, Mahindra, Tata investing heavily
**Indian Examples**: Ola's autonomous division, IIT research
**Technical Deep Dive**: Computer vision for Indian roads, edge AI

### Episode 114: Green Computing & Sustainability
**Focus**: Carbon-neutral data centers, energy optimization
**Why Important**: India's net-zero commitment by 2070
**Indian Examples**: Infosys carbon-neutral campus, renewable energy
**Technical Deep Dive**: PUE optimization, cooling innovations

### Episode 115: Hyperautomation & RPA
**Focus**: Intelligent process automation, cognitive RPA
**Why Important**: IT services transformation
**Indian Examples**: TCS Cognix, Infosys AssistEdge, Wipro HOLMES
**Technical Deep Dive**: ML-powered automation, process mining

### Episode 116: Spatial Computing & AR/VR
**Focus**: Metaverse platforms, industrial AR
**Why Important**: $40B market opportunity by 2030
**Indian Examples**: Jio Glass, Flipkart's virtual shopping
**Technical Deep Dive**: WebXR, spatial anchors, SLAM algorithms

### Episode 117: Confidential Computing
**Focus**: Secure enclaves, homomorphic encryption
**Why Important**: Data privacy regulations, zero-trust evolution
**Indian Examples**: Banking sector adoption, Aadhaar security
**Technical Deep Dive**: Intel SGX, AMD SEV, ARM TrustZone

### Episode 118: Network Function Virtualization (NFV)
**Focus**: 5G core networks, edge computing integration
**Why Important**: Jio, Airtel's 5G rollout
**Indian Examples**: Jio's cloud-native 5G, Airtel edge cloud
**Technical Deep Dive**: Container-based VNFs, network slicing

### Episode 119: Cognitive Search & Knowledge Graphs
**Focus**: Enterprise search, semantic understanding
**Why Important**: Information explosion in enterprises
**Indian Examples**: Flipkart's product graph, Zomato's food ontology
**Technical Deep Dive**: Graph embeddings, vector databases

### Episode 120: India Stack 2.0 - The Future
**Focus**: Next generation of India's digital infrastructure
**Why Important**: Global adoption of Indian DPI model
**Indian Examples**: ONDC, Account Aggregator, Health Stack
**Technical Deep Dive**: Interoperability, privacy-preserving tech
